What no other tool answers

How it works

Three steps, no setup.

01

Paste an address

Any wallet or smart account, any of five networks. Nothing is installed and no wallet is connected, so you can inspect an address you do not own.

02

Read the reconstruction

Approvals are read live from each token contract, outflows are paired with their submitting address, and the coverage panel states exactly what was and was not read.

03

Revoke, and verify

Connect the wallet that owns the address and revoke any permission. After the receipt, the allowance is read back — a surviving allowance is reported as still allowed, never as a green tick.

What this cannot see

The limits are part of the product.

ERC-20 allowances only. ERC-721 and ERC-1155 approvals are not covered.
Off-chain permits. Permit2, ERC-2612 and Seaport signatures leave no trace on chain until they are redeemed.
A bounded history. Approval events are read over a validated block window; an older permission that never touched the wallet's readable history would be missed.
No threat feed. Anchrion ships no list of malicious addresses, and will not call an address an attacker without on-chain evidence.
