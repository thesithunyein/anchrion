How the pool is split

The pool is fixed at the start of each round. Tiers are percentages of what is left, so an early claim is always worth more than a late one.

How a tier is calculated

Your tier is the highest milestone your approved submission count reached before the round closed. Submissions still in review at close count toward the next round, not this one.

When payouts land

Claiming moves the amount to your balance immediately. Withdrawals settle within one business day, and the pool updates the moment a claim is made.

What disqualifies a submission

Duplicate geometry, scans you did not take, or a model already uploaded under a different account. A rejected submission does not reduce your milestone count from earlier approvals.

Rules — monthly milestones

One account, one count. Approved submissions are counted per account. Re-uploading a model you already submitted does not increase your count.

The pool is fixed per round. It starts at $1,000,000 and falls as creators claim. Tier values are shown up front and do not change mid-round.

Your tier is kept for the round in which you reached it. Reaching a tier on the last day pays the same as reaching it on the first.

Claims are final once they settle. A claimed chest cannot be unclaimed, so claim after your last submission of the round if you are close to the next tier.
