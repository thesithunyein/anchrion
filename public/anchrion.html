How it works

Three steps, no setup.

01

Paste an address

Five networks, no wallet, no install. Inspect an address you don't own.

02

Read the reconstruction

Approvals read live from each token contract. Outflows paired with whoever submitted them.

03

Revoke, then verify

Sign the revoke. The allowance is read back — still allowed if it survives, never a green tick.

What this cannot see

The limits are part of the product.

ERC-20 allowances only. Not ERC-721 or ERC-1155 approvals.
Off-chain permits. Permit2, ERC-2612 and Seaport stay invisible until redeemed.
A bounded history. Permissions older than the readable block window are missed.
No threat feed. Nothing is called an attacker without on-chain evidence.
